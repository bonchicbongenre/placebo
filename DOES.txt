What doesthisdoanything.click does. A golf pass must keep every line true.

 1. One corner, seen whole on the first screen: a pedestrian signal, and beside it
    the sign PUSH BUTTON FOR WALK SIGNAL over its push button.
 2. The signal is two lenses of LEDs, 33 by 33: an upraised hand above, in amber,
    and a walking person below, in Ding white. The symbols are the US standard ones
    (MUTCD), sampled in whole LEDs. Unlit, each shows as a faint grid of its LEDs;
    lit, it blooms a little. Without script the symbols are solid.
 3. Everyone, anywhere, sees the same corner at the same moment: the signal keeps
    the world's clock. Each cycle is 45 seconds: the hand, until the walking person
    comes, somewhere from 4 to 27 seconds in, as the traffic has it; the walking
    person for 7 seconds; then the hand, flashing once a second, for 11. With
    reduced motion the hand stays lit instead of flashing.
 4. A press (click, Space or Enter) lights the button's lamp until this corner's
    next walking person, and changes when that walking person comes, within what
    the traffic allows. The button gives under the press; the lamp answers within
    a frame. Nothing about a press is kept.
 5. A screen reader hears "Wait." at every press, and "Walk sign is on." when the
    walking person answers a press. The signal is labelled DONT WALK, WALK, or
    DONT WALK, flashing. A text-only reader sees DONT WALK and WALK, the sign, and
    the label.
 6. The label gives the page's own size, read live, and one line: "Wait." The
    title (the tab) is "Push button for walk signal."; a link's preview adds
    "Wait." The console gives a Latin word, and what it means.
 7. Colours come from the house tokens: amber, and gold for the signal's housing;
    without the tokens every colour falls to black, white or a grey. The housing is
    gold by day and black by night; the lights and the lamp keep their own colours
    in both themes. Dark mode follows the system until the theme button ("dark" or
    "light", beside the label) turns it for the visit; nothing is kept. A change of
    theme fades over half a second.
 8. The 404 is the sign, with no button under it.
 9. The favicon is the button from the front, in amber, lighter on dark tab bars.
